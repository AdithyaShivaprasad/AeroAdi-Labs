About

Why AeroAdi Labs exists

Most aviation content online falls into two camps: encyclopedic walls of numbers, or shallow listicles. AeroAdi Labs is a bet that there's a third way — interactive, honest, and beautiful — where you don't just read that wings make lift, you drag a slider and watch a wing stall.

Who builds this

AeroAdi Labs is an independent student project — built by one aviation-obsessed high school senior, with a lot of care and no corporate backing. It exists for anyone who looks up when a plane flies over.

How the numbers are handled

Aircraft specifications vary by variant, configuration, and source. Our policy: every figure is a typical published value for the named variant, drawn from manufacturer data and public records, labeled with units, and marked with a note when a figure needs context (like combat radius versus ferry range). When sources disagree, we choose the most commonly cited figure rather than the most impressive one. Passenger traffic figures are recent annual totals, rounded.

What this site will never do

No ads, no dark patterns, no fake urgency, no invented facts. If something is uncertain, it says so. If something is planned but not built (live weather, maps, accounts), the site tells you honestly rather than pretending.

Roadmap

Coming in future updates: more aircraft and airports every month, an airline directory, deeper learning paths (navigation, weather, how airlines work), a world route map, and optional accounts to sync progress across devices. Progress currently lives privately in your browser.

Corrections

Spotted an error? That's a gift — the fastest way to make the site better. A feedback channel is coming with the next update; for now, corrections reach us through the project's GitHub repository.
